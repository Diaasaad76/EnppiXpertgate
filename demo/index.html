Project Objectives

The 6 Main Goals of XpertGate

Goal 01

Make the Best Use of Our Own Experts

Identify and qualify top professionals across petroleum companies to share knowledge and deliver training through the ENPPI Training Center.

Goal 02

Turn Field Experience into Practical Training

Close the gap between theory and real site work by turning technical standards and field lessons into practical training courses.

Goal 03

Keep Critical Knowledge Inside the Sector

Protect valuable company know-how from being lost to retirement by involving senior and retired experts in training younger staff.

Goal 04

Build an Approved Trainers Directory

Create one central, easy-to-use directory of certified trainers and consultants so all sector companies can find the right expert quickly.

Goal 05

Strengthen ENPPI's Role as a Training Leader

Support ENPPI's role as the Ministry's trusted engineering and training center for developing modern energy skills.

Goal 06

Reduce Training Costs

Rely less on costly foreign training providers and deliver timely training tailored to our operations.
